Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and the choices you have. Last updated 8 October 2026.

Tools

Calculators run entirely in your browser. Numbers, names and dates you type into a tool are not sent to us or stored.

Forms

When you submit a form (concierge, contact, newsletter, contests, donations, careers), we receive the details you enter, the page you sent it from and basic referral information. Forms are delivered to our team by email through the FormSubmit relay service. We use these details only to answer your request and, if you opt in, to send the newsletter. We never sell personal data.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings and learn more at how Google uses data. Where required by law, a consent message is shown before personalised ads.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful. It sets cookies and collects anonymised usage data.

Videos

Embedded videos load from youtube-nocookie.com only when you press play.

Browser storage

We keep a few session values (for example, whether you’ve already seen a pop-up) in your browser’s session storage; they disappear when you close the tab.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal details you sent us, or to stop contacting you, through the contact form. Newsletter emails include an unsubscribe link.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and contests are open only to adults.

Changes

We’ll update this page if our practices change and revise the date above.
