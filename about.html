About 115522

An independent reference for what numbers mean, what they’re worth and how different cultures read them.

People care about numbers for very different reasons. Some notice 11:11 and wonder why. Some are choosing a phone number to match their birth date, or bidding for a plate. Some invest in numeric domains. Until now, each group had its own sites — and none of them told the whole story. 115522 does: meaning, math, culture and value, on one page, for any number.

How we work

  • We show our working. Every calculation is explained; scores list each point.
  • We separate belief from fact. Numerology and luck are presented as traditions; math is math; prices come from public, linked sources.
  • We cite sources. Guides and records link to official pages, auction results and reputable reporting.
  • We correct mistakes. Spot one? Tell us and we’ll fix it.

How we make money

Display advertising (Google AdSense), sponsorships, the paid side of the number concierge, and reader donations. Sponsors never decide editorial content, and sponsored placements are always labelled.
