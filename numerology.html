Numerology systems compared

Pythagorean, Chaldean and Vedic numerology use different letter values and rules. Here is how each one works, side by side.

Numerology assigns meaning to numbers and converts names and dates into numbers. Three systems dominate: the Pythagorean (Western) system, the older Chaldean system, and Vedic (Indian) numerology, which links each digit to a planet. They often give different answers for the same name — which is why our tools show more than one.
