The triad method

A common approach groups numbers into three families: 1, 5, 7 (independent, mental), 2, 4, 8 (practical, material) and 3, 6, 9 (creative, emotional). Numbers in the same family are read as natural matches; neighbouring pairings as workable; others as growth relationships. Master numbers are compared through their base (11 → 2, 22 → 4, 33 → 6). Published charts differ, so treat this as one lens among many.
