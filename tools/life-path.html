Life path number calculator

Your life path is numerology’s core number, worked out from your full date of birth. We show every step.

How the life path is calculated

Reduce the month, the day and the year separately to single digits — keeping 11, 22 and 33 whole — then add the three results and reduce again. For 22 May 1990: month 5; day 22 (a master number, kept); year 1+9+9+0 = 19 → 10 → 1. Then 5 + 22 + 1 = 28 → 10 → 1. Reducing each part first is the method most numerologists use, because adding every digit at once can hide master numbers.

Questions

Which is more important, life path or name number?

Most numerologists treat the life path as the core number because a birth date can’t change, while a name can.

What are master numbers?

11, 22 and 33. They are not reduced to 2, 4 and 6 and are read as intensified versions of those numbers.

What is a personal year?

Your birth month and day plus the current year, reduced. It describes the theme of the year in a nine-year cycle.
