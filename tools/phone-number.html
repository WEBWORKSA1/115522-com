How a mobile number is read

Indian numerologists add all ten digits and reduce the total, keeping 11, 22 and 33. The result is matched against your birth number (your day of birth reduced) and your life path. Totals of 1, 3, 5, 6 and 9 are often recommended for business owners; many people avoid totals of 4 and 8 unless their chart supports them. Chinese buyers look at the digits themselves: eights add value, fours subtract it, and endings like 88 or 168 are prized.
