Vehicle number plate checker

Read any number plate three ways — registration number, all digits, and letters plus digits — and see if it suits you.

Which digits count?

There are three common methods. Most Indian numerologists use only the final registration number (the last four digits). Some add every digit on the plate, including the state and RTO codes. Chaldean practitioners also convert the letters to numbers. We calculate all three so you can see whether they agree.

Getting a fancy number in India

Choice and fancy numbers are sold through state transport e-auctions on the Parivahan portal, with reserve prices that rise with demand — 0001 carries the highest reserve. Our step-by-step auction guide explains registration, bidding and payment deadlines. In the UAE and Hong Kong, plates are sold at government and licensed auctions; see the record sales.

Questions

Which plate total is lucky?

Numerologists match the plate total to the owner’s birth number; totals of 1, 5 and 6 are popular general choices, and many avoid 4 and 8.

Do letters on a plate count?

In the Chaldean method, yes. Most Indian practitioners use only the digits.

Can I transfer a fancy number to a new car?

Rules differ by country and state; India, the UAE and Hong Kong each have their own transfer rules.
