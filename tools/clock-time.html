Keep seeing the same time? Enter it — or press “Use the time now” — to see what kind of time it is and how it’s read.

Mirror, reversed and repeating times

Mirror hours Hours and minutes match: 11:11, 12:12, 22:22.
Reversed hours The time reads the same backwards: 12:21, 10:01, 23:32.
Repeating digits Three or four of the same digit: 11:11, 22:22, 00:00.
Sequences Ascending or descending runs: 12:34, 23:45.

Questions

What does 11:11 mean?

It’s the best-known mirror hour, read as a moment of alignment or a prompt to set an intention.

Why do I keep seeing the same time?

Habit and attention: you check your phone at similar times, and patterns stand out once they feel meaningful.

Do 24-hour clocks change the meaning?

They change which times you can see — 22:22 only appears on a 24-hour display.
