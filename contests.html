Rules

  1. Entry is free; no purchase necessary. One entry per person per contest per month.
  2. Entrants must be 18 or older. Void where prohibited by law.
  3. Guess the Hammer uses the publicly reported final price of the featured auction; if it’s not reported within 14 days, the contest rolls over.
  4. Story and video entries must be your own original work. By entering, you allow 115522 to publish your entry with credit; you keep your copyright.
  5. Judging is by the 115522 editorial team; decisions are final. Winners are contacted by email and must reply within 14 days.
  6. Prizes are paid by PayPal or bank transfer. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
  7. We may change or cancel a contest if a sponsor withdraws or the featured auction is cancelled; entries then roll over.
